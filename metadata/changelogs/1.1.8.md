# 1.1.8

## Changes

- Datapack and KubeJS recipes for Neo Vitae's own crafting stations can now be written once and used on both the 1.21.1 and the 26.1 builds. Both the older and the newer way of writing ingredients is accepted on either version.
- Athanor recipes no longer have to list their guaranteed and chanced outputs. Either list can be left out, so a recipe that only produces a fluid is now possible.

## Fixes

- The Athanor recipe examples in the pack maker docs used field names that do not exist, so recipes copied from them would never load. They now match the real format, and the full list of fields is documented.
- The Torment Nexus starts holding back spawns the moment its chunk loads, so mobs no longer get a few seconds of freedom after you log back in.
- Ender IO Powered Spawners bound to the Torment Nexus now actually produce loot and Essentia Vitae, and stop reading as "Blocked by another mod".

## Translations

- Translation updates across most supported languages, including a large Simplified Chinese update.
