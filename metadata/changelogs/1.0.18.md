# 1.0.18

## Additions

- The Seer's Sigil now reveals a ritual's working area. While you carry one, looking at an active Master Ritual Stone outlines each of the ritual's configured ranges in the world with a coloured box. The sigil only needs to be on you, it works from your inventory, from inside a Sigil of Holding, or worn in a Curios slot.

## Changes

- The Ritual Reader has been renamed to the Ritual Configurator (same item, same recipe, same function, new name). Its in-game name, guide book, and wiki entries are all updated to match.
- The Ritual Configurator can now relocate a ritual's chest, tank, or input/output container instead of it always sitting directly above the Master Ritual Stone. In the Define Area mode, Sneak + Use in the air to cycle to the chest/tank zone, then mark two opposite corners to move it. This also fixes zone-cycling, which previously couldn't reach anything past a ritual's first zone. The Endless Quarry, Hymn of Siphoning, and the Torment Nexus now honour the relocated container too (they used to force it directly above the stone).
