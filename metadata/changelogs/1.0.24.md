# 1.0.24

## Fixes

- Fixed the Athanor's slot tooltips appearing over your own inventory. Hovering an empty inventory slot could show "Fluid Output" or other Athanor slot hints; those hints now only show on the Athanor's own slots.
- Active rituals now re-check their structure and stop if a ritual stone has been removed. Previously you could mine out the surrounding ritual stones and the ritual would keep running; it now ends once its layout is no longer intact.
