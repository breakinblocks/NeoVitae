# 1.1.1

## Additions

- Operators can now summon a meteor on demand with `/neovitae meteor <pos> <catalyst>`: choose the catalyst item and the coordinates it starts falling from. It craters on impact by default, or you can pass optional detonation coordinates to burst it at a set height.

## Fixes

- Anointments (oils and alkahests) now apply to pickaxes, axes, shovels, hoes and other tools again, not just swords.
- Anointments can now also be applied in a smithing table, as the guide describes: put the anointment in the template slot and your tool or weapon in the base slot. This is the way to anoint two-handed weapons that can't spare an offhand.
- The Vas Maleficum now shows the item held inside it, floating above the vessel.
- Descending to a dungeon's lower floors no longer boots you out; you are only returned to the overworld if you actually fall out of the world.
