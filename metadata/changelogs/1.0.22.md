# 1.0.22

## Additions

- Brilliance for Living Armour can now be crafted as an Upgrade Tome in the Tabula Vitae from a diamond, a written book, a netherite ingot, and a shulker shell (needs a Master Blood Orb and 10,000 Essentia Vitae). Apply the tome to your armour to gain a level of Brilliance, up to four.

## Changes

- The Living Armour upgrade that used to show as "Forgotten" is now called "Brilliance" everywhere, matching the guide and wiki.

## Fixes

- The Foreman's treasure now drops a usable Upgrade Tome (Curios Sockets, Elytra, or Gilded) instead of a blank one that granted nothing when applied.
- Applying Blood Mending at the Hellfire Forge now requires a minimum of 400 Spiritus and consumes 200. The two values were swapped, so the forge asked for only 200 to start but tried to drain 400.
