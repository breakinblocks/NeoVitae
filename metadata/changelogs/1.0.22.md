# 1.0.22

## Changes

- The Lex Vitae's beam now fires from a rebindable key instead of holding right-click, so right-click is free for your off-hand. You can now raise the Orb of Vitae's shield in your off-hand while wielding an active Lex Vitae. The key is unbound by default; assign it under Controls > Neo Vitae > "Lex Vitae: Fire Beam". Toggling the Lex on and off is still sneak + right-click.
- The Orb of Vitae's shield is now on a rebindable key (Controls > Neo Vitae > "Blood Shield"), bound to right-click by default so it works as before. Because it no longer relies on the right-click item action, it can be held at the same time as the Lex Vitae beam, and you can rebind it off right-click if you prefer. Raising it still needs an Orb of Vitae in your off-hand.
- The ore-processing items for iron, gold, copper, demonite, and netherite (fragments, gravels, and dusts) now use their own hand-drawn textures instead of the generic color-tinted ones. Other materials keep the tinted look.
- Updated the artwork for the Ritual Diviner and Ritual Diviner [Dusk].
- The Daemonium and Slime of Vitae spawn eggs now use custom artwork instead of the plain coloured spawn egg.
- The blood fill on the Veneficus, Magus, and Dominus Orbs of Vitae now lines up with their updated artwork.
- The Lex Vitae beam now has a mode toggle, bound to L by default (rebind under Controls > Neo Vitae > "Lex Vitae: Cycle Mode"). It cycles between Mining, Damage, and Both: in Mining mode the beam ignores creatures, in Damage mode it ignores blocks, and Both does everything. It starts in Both mode, and the current mode shows in the tooltip.
- Added a Lex Vitae entry to the guide book, covering its modes, the beam, mining radius, and inventory pickup.
- The guide book can now be placed on a lectern and slotted into a chiseled bookshelf.

## Fixes

- The Seer Sigil now reveals an altar's or ritual's extra information from anywhere on your person, as the guide describes: your full inventory, inside a Sigil of Holding, or worn in a Curios slot. It previously only worked from your hotbar or off-hand.
- Spiritus souls worth nothing no longer drop. Killing a very low-health creature (such as the smallest slimes) could leave behind a soul holding 0 Spiritus; those kills now simply drop no soul instead.
- GeckoLib and Modonomicon are now marked as required dependencies on CurseForge and Modrinth, so launchers download them automatically with the mod. They were always required to run, but were listed as optional (or not at all), which could leave the game unable to start.
- The Lex Vitae beam sends the blocks it mines straight into your inventory, so nothing is lost at the far end of its range.
- Raw Spiritus now shows the correct artwork for each aspect (Ruina, Nihilum, Vindicta, Invictus, and Raw), matching its Spiritus soul. It was displaying leftover placeholder textures.
