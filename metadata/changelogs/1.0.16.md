# 1.0.16

## Changes

- The Cry of the Forsaken Soul ritual is now called The Ritual of Lost Souls, matching the name used in the guide book and wiki.
- Runes, Bloodstones, and the capstone blocks used to build higher-tier altars can now be referenced as a group by recipes, automation, and other mods, the same way their placed forms already could.
- Dungeon Ore has been renamed to Demonite Ore.

## Fixes

- Demonite Ore now drops Raw Demonite when mined, or the ore block itself with Silk Touch. It was dropping both the ore block and Raw Demonite at once, ignoring Silk Touch.
- The Tabula Vitae now consumes its ingredients when crafting. Recipes were finishing and producing their output without using up the materials placed in the table.
- Breaking a Tabula Vitae no longer voids its contents. Items left inside are now dropped no matter which part of the table you break.
- The Tabula Vitae recipe pages in JEI now show the correct Blood Orb. Recipes that need an Apprentice Orb were showing the lower Weak Orb, which the table would not accept.
- Curios slots now accept their items again. The Sentient Training Bracelet, Tome of Peritia, sigils, Spiritus Gems, and the Spiritus Aura Gauge could not be placed into their charm, bracelet, or necklace slots; they can now all be equipped.
- Upgrade Tomes found in mine chests now carry their intended Living Armor upgrade and experience. They were dropping blank, with no upgrade attached and nothing to apply when used.
