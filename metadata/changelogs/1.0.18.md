# 1.0.18

## Additions

- Earn a new advancement for imbuing a tool or piece of armor with Blood Mending at the Hellfire Forge.

## Fixes

- The Ara Vitae once again pulses a redstone signal when a transmutation finishes, so a redstone lamp or noteblock placed directly beneath the altar reacts as the guide describes.
- The Torment Nexus no longer drains your Soul Network uncontrollably. The EV cost to run it is now capped per operation and measured against your live balance, so even a sprawling spawner array can only ask so much of your network at once. Kills past that point still happen, still drop their loot, still grant experience, and still feed their sacrifice EV into the altar.
- Regeneration and Poison flask recipes now show their real base duration when previewed. The lengthening and potency recipes had been displaying a placeholder three-minute version that didn't match the flask you can actually brew.
- Ritual previews in the guide book now rotate around the Master Ritual Stone at their center, instead of spinning off to one side.
