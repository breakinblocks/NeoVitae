# 1.1.28

## Changes

- The two Spirit Caches at the start of the Endless Realm now always hold 2 to 3 Standard Dungeon Keys each.
- Trial spawners in the Endless Realm, including The Foreman's, now have a 50% chance to drop a Standard Dungeon Key when cleared.
- Chests in the Endless Realm are more likely to contain a Standard Dungeon Key.
