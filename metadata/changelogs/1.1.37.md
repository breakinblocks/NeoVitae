# 1.1.37

## Changes

- Every decorative Dungeon block can now be crafted in all five aspects: bricks, tiles, pillars, pillar caps, slabs, stairs, walls, gates and Dungeon Eyes.
- Dungeon Stone spreads: craft one Dungeon Stone with three Stone to get four Dungeon Stone of the same aspect.
- Any Dungeon block of an aspect can be cut into any other in the Stonecutter.
- JEI has a Dungeon Stone Spreading page for each aspect, showing the recipe and an animation of the stone spreading.

## Fixes

- Dungeon Metal can be cut from a Hellforged Block or from another aspect's Dungeon Metal in the Stonecutter, and crafted back into nine Hellforged Ingots, as the guide describes.
- Swapping out a piece of Sentient Armor no longer strips the armor value and other stats from a chestplate from another mod, such as the Quantum Chestplate from Modern Industrialization or chestplates from All The Arcanist Gear (breakinblocks/NeoVitae#178).
