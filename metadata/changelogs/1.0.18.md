# 1.0.18

## Additions

- Earn a new advancement for imbuing a tool or piece of armor with Blood Mending at the Hellfire Forge.

## Fixes

- The Ara Vitae once again pulses a redstone signal when a transmutation finishes, so a redstone lamp or noteblock placed directly beneath the altar reacts as the guide describes.
- The Torment Nexus no longer drains your Soul Network uncontrollably. The EV cost to run it is now capped per operation, so even a sprawling spawner array can only ask so much of your network at once. Kills past that point still happen, still drop their loot, still grant experience, and still feed their sacrifice EV into the altar.
- Torment Nexus kills now bank their EV through the altar itself, charging your bound Blood Orb at the altar's normal rate rather than bypassing it, so its output respects your altar's runes and capacity.
