# Daemonium Bestiary

> *Know your enemy. Then harvest it.*

The corridors of the [Endless Dungeon](The-Endless-Dungeon) are patrolled by a roster of ten custom demons, collectively the **Daemonium**. They appear from trial spawners scaled by player count, drop unique trophy materials, and align with one or more of the five [Spiritus](Spiritus-Aspects-and-Crystals) aspects.

## Shared Behaviours

Every Daemonium shares a handful of mechanical tells.

- **Spawn invulnerability.** For the first 50 ticks (2.5 seconds) after spawning, a Daemonium plays a spawn animation and cannot be damaged. Trial spawners drop their occupants directly on top of you; do not waste your first sigil charge.
- **Attack self-stun.** Heavy attacks freeze the demon's movement for the duration of the animation. This is your tell and your window.
- **Layered sound design.** Spawn, attack, hurt, and death events stack several vanilla sounds at once. The audio is the cleanest indicator of which attack is incoming.

## Fodder Tier

### Daemonium Cruoris, The Blood Demon

30 HP, 5 damage. Three attacks: **left claw**, **right claw**, and a **leap attack** that closes a 3 to 10 block gap at 1.5× damage. The leap fires roughly 40% of the time at edge-of-melee range; bait it, sidestep it, and the demon eats 75 ticks of self-stun. Its walk/run stance shifts visibly when committing to a charge.

**Drops:** Gore-Clotted Fang, Tainted Flesh, Raw / Spiritus Nihilum.

### Daemonium Pestis, The Pestilence Spider

30 HP, 6 damage, negligible armour. Two attacks: a **fang bite** and a **shadow lunge** that dashes through the gap and bites again at the far end. Both inflict **Poison II for 5 seconds**. Individually fragile, but they swarm; the poison stack will kill you faster than the bites do. Bring milk or a [flask](Tabula-Vitae-Flasks-and-Anointments).

**Drops:** Venomgland Sac, Tainted Flesh, Ruina / Raw Spiritus, Spider Eye.

### Daemonium Rancoris, The Spite Phantom

The dungeon's common ranged threat. Most archery-flavoured trial spawns produce Rancoris; roughly one in ten produces a Slime of Vitae instead.

Spectral ranged attacker, 45 HP, 6 damage. Two attacks: a **spectral bolt** (single tracking projectile, Slowness I for 3s) and an **ectoplasmic burst** (AoE at the target's feet, 1.5× damage, Slowness II for 5s). Will kite across the room; close the gap or you take the burst point-blank.

**Drops:** Ectoplasmic Residue, Tainted Flesh, Vindicta / Spiritus Invictus.

### Daemonium Animaris, The Soul Wraith

Flying, partly-phasing, 30 HP, 7 damage. Drifts through walls and through your guard alike, charging straight through targets and reorienting on the other side. Awkward to hit cleanly. Its low health is the main mercy.

**Drops:** Animus Mote, Invictus / Spiritus Vindicta, Phantom Membrane (rare).

### Slime of Vitae

Sometimes summoned in place of Rancoris from the same trial spawners.

A blood-red slime of coagulated [Essentia Vitae](Blood-Orbs-and-Anima). Touch slows; **death detonation deals raw magic damage in an AoE**. Magic-immune; bring physical attacks. If **four small slimes gather**, they merge into a larger variant. Kill them faster than they clump.

## Standard Tier

### Daemonium Voraxis, The Voracious Oni

60 HP, 8 damage, 6 armour, 0.28 movement. The first demon that genuinely tanks. Two attacks: **left slash** and **right slash**, both apply **Hunger II for 5 seconds**. Extended Voraxis fights force you to eat mid-combat, which is exactly when their friends close in.

**Drops:** Hollow Gut, Tainted Flesh, Nihilum / Spiritus Ruina, Raw Demonite (rare).

---

## Elite Tier

### Daemonium Corrodis, The Wither Knight

100 HP, 8 damage, **15 armour**, 0.28 movement. Three attacks: a **sweep**, a **double sweep**, and a two-phase **slam** with wider arc. **Every hit applies Wither**, and the slam adds Weakness for 5 seconds. Armour-piercing weapons or anointments are the cleanest counter.

**Drops:** Blight Marrow, Raw Demonite, Ruina / Spiritus Vindicta, Wither Skeleton Skull (rare).

### Daemonium Fervidis, The Undying Brute

150 HP, 12 damage, 10 armour. Three modes: **decay swing** (basic melee), **revenant smash** (leap slam at 1.5× damage), and the **bear stance**, a defensive posture granting **Resistance II for 2.5s and near-invulnerability**. Wait the stance out; do not waste cooldowns into it.

**Drops:** Revenant Plate, Gore-Clotted Fang (rare), Raw Demonite, Nihilum / Spiritus Invictus.

### Daemonium Doloris, The Wendigo of Pain

Also the species of the dungeon boss; see "The Foreman" below.

200 HP, 10 damage, 8 armour, 0.24 movement, **two-phase**. Phase 1 cycles **Combo 1**, **Combo 2**, and a **leap slam** at 1.5× damage. Below 50% HP, **Phase 2** opens **Combo 3** (upward slash + spin, applies **Wither II**) and the **ghost howl**, briefly phasing Doloris out of reality before counter-attacking. The phase transition has a distinct sound layer; back off when you hear it.

**Drops:** Frozen Marrow Shard, Ectoplasmic Residue (rare), Raw Demonite, Vindicta / Spiritus Nihilum.

#### The Foreman

The Foreman is a unique Daemonium Doloris variant that spawns once per dungeon, inside the sealed Foreman's Key chamber. Vital statistics:

- **600 HP**, **25 damage**, **16 armour**, 8 armour toughness.
- Hosts a vanilla **boss bar** while alive.
- On death, drops the **Foreman's Key**, which opens the sealed mine passage.

Treat it as a boss fight, not an elite encounter. Bring [sigils](Sigils) and [sentient weaponry](Hellfire-Forge-and-Sentient-Equipment); attempt it under-equipped at your peril.

## Apex Tier

### Daemonium Ignis, The Fire Demon

60 HP, 6 damage, 7 armour, fire-immune, permanent Slow Falling. Three attacks chosen by range and arc:

- **Fireball** at long range: Large Fireball at 1.5× velocity.
- **Ground Slam** when in front and within 4.2 blocks: AoE at your feet, ignites for 5s.
- **Sword Slash** at other ranges below 25 blocks: **Slowness IV for 3s**, then **four hits over 16 ticks**, each resetting your i-frames and refreshing fire. The slowness locks you in the multi-hit.

Stay off-arc when close. Stay behind cover when far. Anywhere in between is the kill zone.

**Drops:** Cinder Heart Fragment, Raw Demonite, Nihilum / Ruina Spiritus, Blaze Rod (rare).

### Daemonium Glaciaris, The Ice Colossus

The single most dangerous creature in the dungeon.

250 HP, 10 base damage, 8 armour, 0.2 movement. Six attacks:

- **Basic ice projectile**: 10 damage, Slowness II for 2s.
- **Ice Beam**: channelled ~5s, 0.25 damage/tick + Slowness III refresh. Locks disengagement.
- **Rose Toss**: thrown ice rose, 10 damage, Slowness III for 3s.
- **Shards Burst**: close-range AoE, 15 damage, Slowness IV for 3s.
- **Wall Summon**: temporary ice wall and ice golem as area denial.
- **Mist Phase**: 20 seconds of full invulnerability while repositioning.

Drops every variety of Spiritus and the rare **Permafrost Core** required for the most powerful [Hellfire Forge](Hellfire-Forge-and-Sentient-Equipment) recipes, including the **Sigil of the Damned**. Plan the fight around the mist phase.

**Drops:** Permafrost Core, Frozen Marrow Shard (rare), Raw Demonite, **all Spiritus types**.
