# 1.1.53

## Additions

- The Sanguine Crafter has a button to turn chunk loading off for that crafter. breakinblocks/NeoVitae#236

## Fixes

- The Endless Quarry and Deus Endless Quarry now restart from the top when the block beneath the master stone changes, so a bigger foundation covers the full new area right away.
- The Deus Endless Quarry clears four times as much rock per pulse in Clear rock mode, matching its ore speed.
- The Deus Endless Quarry's ritual info and guide page now mention that a Sanguine Deus Block gives it a 511x511 area.
- Self-sacrifice (blood orbs, ceremonial sacrifice with incense, the altar drawing blood and the Feathered Knife ritual) can no longer kill you, even when other mods increase the damage you take. FTBTeam/FTB-Modpack-Issues#13986
- Enchanted gear in the mines chests no longer rolls enchantments that are meant to stay out of random loot. breakinblocks/NeoVitae#238
- The arrays that rise above the capstones of a Tier 5 and Tier 6 Ara Vitae while it crafts now show up on servers, not just in singleplayer. breakinblocks/NeoVitae#237
