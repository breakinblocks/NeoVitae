# 1.0.23

## Changes

- The Tree Felling ritual now replants a sapling where each tree stood, using the saplings that tree dropped. If no sapling is available the spot is simply left clear, as before.

## Fixes

- Fixed the Athanor's slot tooltips appearing over your own inventory. Hovering an empty inventory slot could show "Fluid Output" or other Athanor slot hints; those hints now only show on the Athanor's own slots.
- Active rituals now re-check their structure and stop if a ritual stone has been removed. Previously you could mine out the surrounding ritual stones and the ritual would keep running; it now ends once its layout is no longer intact.

## Compatibility

- Added support for Sable (Aeronautics) airships and build plots. Rituals and the Ara Vitae now behave correctly when built on a moving ship or inside a build plot:
  - The Ara Vitae draws blood from nearby players and self-sacrifice works again near an altar on a ship.
  - The Ritual Reader and configuration tools now find ritual stones on ships and plots.
  - The Collection ritual deposits its harvest into the linked storage instead of dropping it far away.
  - The Speed ritual launches entities in the direction the ship is actually facing.
  - The Meteor ritual lands its meteor on the ship rather than high above the world.
  - Ritual particle effects now appear at the ship instead of streaming off into the distance.
