# 1.1.11

## Changes

- New ritual: the Ritual of Enchanted Vitae. Throw an item and any number of enchanted books onto the master stone and it binds every enchantment the item can take, handing the books back unspent.
- The ritual keeps no level limit of its own, so a Sharpness 99 book binds Sharpness 99. It charges 2,000 Essentia Vitae per level bound, more for the rarer enchantments.
- Two enchantments that cannot share an item stop the ritual. The books at fault glow for ten seconds, and it will not run again until you take one away and offer the item back.

## Fixes

- Neo Vitae and Deeper and Darker compat added.
- Fixed Flask and TV recipes display in the guide book
