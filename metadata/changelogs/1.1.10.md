# 1.1.10

## Changes

- The Array of Liquified Experience now moves as much as it can each second. It fills the tank as far as the tank will take it, and empties the tank just as quickly when powered, instead of trickling a little at a time.

## Fixes

- The Tome of Peritia window is wider, so large amounts no longer run past the edges of the panel.
- The Tome of Peritia now updates its stored total as you click, instead of only showing the new figure after you close and reopen it.
