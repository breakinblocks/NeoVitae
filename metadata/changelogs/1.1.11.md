# 1.1.11

## Changes

- New ritual: the Ritual of Enchanted Vitae. Throw an item and any number of enchanted books onto the master stone and it binds every enchantment the item can take, handing the books back unspent.
- The ritual keeps no level limit of its own, so a Sharpness 99 book binds Sharpness 99. It charges 2,000 Essentia Vitae per level bound, more for the rarer enchantments.
- Two enchantments that cannot share an item stop the ritual. The books at fault glow for ten seconds, and it will not run again until you take one away and offer the item back.
- Packs can set the Ritual of Enchanted Vitae to spend the books it reads instead of returning them. Off by default, so books are kept unless your pack turns it on.
- The Ritual of the Mason builds far faster and far bigger. Raw Spiritus in the chunk raises it from one block a pulse to eight, and its area from 5,000 blocks up to 80,000. The aura is spent as it works, so it settles at whatever pace your supply sustains.
- The Ritual of the Mason can be told what shape to build: solid, a hollow shell, a floor, walls, a roof, or a bare frame. Pick one in the Ritual Configurator.
- Holding the Ritual Configurator while carrying a Seer's Sigil outlines the working area of every master stone near you, whether or not the ritual has been activated.

## Fixes

- Neo Vitae and Deeper and Darker compat added.
- Fixed Flask and TV recipes display in the guide book
- The Hellfire Forge draws from the gem in its gem primary slot first now.
- Ritual pages in the guide book quoted the wrong running cost for several rituals, it now properly references the datapack values dynamically.
- The Ritual of the Mason no longer destroys blocks that cannot stay where it puts them. A chest of torches used to be consumed a block at a time with nothing to show for it.
- Configured ritual areas survive switching a master stone off and on again. They were being previously reset to the default.
- Everything now calls the Domain of the Filler by one name, the Ritual of the Mason.
