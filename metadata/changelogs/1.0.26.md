# 1.0.26

## Additions

- Diamond, Emerald, Lapis Lazuli, Nether Quartz, and Amethyst now have full material processing chains like the metals. Cut their ore (or Amethyst Clusters) into fragments with a cutting fluid in the Athanor, then work the fragments up through gravel and dust and back into the gem.
- NeoVitae recipes can now be bookmarked in JEI. Open any NeoVitae recipe and use JEI's bookmark button to pin it for quick reference.

## Changes

- The Torment Nexus now keeps suppressing nearby spawners even when it runs out of Essentia Vitae. Its harvesting pauses until EV is available again, but the spawners it covers stay quiet in the meantime.
- The Ritual Diviner's ritual list is now sorted alphabetically by name, so it's easier to find the one you want.

## Fixes

- Fixed a stray texture file whose name caused an "Invalid path in pack" error while loading resources.
- Ritual structure previews placed from the Scriptura Vitae now anchor the block you click on as the Master Ritual Stone, instead of appearing offset up and to the side.
- The Ritual of Harvest now collects sweet berries into its chest instead of dropping them on the ground, and it can now harvest berry bushes from other mods (such as Ars Nouveau Source Berries) as well.
- Holding the FTB Ultimine key near one of your own block-harvesting rituals (such as the Ritual of Harvest or Crystallum Fractura) no longer causes the ritual's harvesting to set off Ultimine and destroy the surrounding field.
- Sigils used as ingredients in Tabula Vitae recipes (such as the Lava Sigil for a lava bucket or the Water Sigil for a water bucket) are no longer consumed when the recipe finishes; they stay behind so you can keep using them.
- The Well of Suffering no longer pours Essentia Vitae straight into your network past your orb's capacity. It now channels the harvested Essentia Vitae into a nearby Ara Vitae, whose orb sets the limit, and it needs an Ara Vitae in range to function.
