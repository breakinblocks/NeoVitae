# 1.0.8

## Fixes

- Fixed every orb-gated recipe being craftable with no Blood Orb at all. The custom orb-tier ingredient reported no matching items while the blood-orb data map was still loading, so the game treated the orb slot as empty (NeoForge's "accidentally empty" ingredient), meaning the slot matched air and a held orb was rejected. The ingredient now always reports its orbs, so recipes such as the Blank Rune, Ritual Stones, runes, Incense Altar, Lava Crystal, Athanor, cells, brick paths, and Experience Tome again require an orb of the correct tier (and the Novicius orb works where it should).
