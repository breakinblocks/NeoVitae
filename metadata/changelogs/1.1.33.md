# 1.1.33

## Fixes

- A bound Lava Crystal now stays bound after burning in a furnace, instead of turning into an unbound crystal after the first item.
- A Lava Crystal now takes 50 EV each time it burns, instead of also draining EV whenever it was placed in a furnace or checked by a hopper.
- Alchemy Flasks can now be rinsed with water as the guide describes: right-click water, a water cauldron or a sink with a drained flask, or craft it with a Water Bucket. Sneak to rinse a flask that still has doses left.
