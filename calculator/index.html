Steady-state EV per second each source puts into this altar, after its sacrifice bonuses. Network cost is what the ritual draws from your Soul Network to keep running. Activation costs are not included.

A bound orb on the altar moves Essentia Vitae from the main tank into its owner's network every tick, up to the network cap. Speed raises the fill rate; Runes of the Orb raise the cap.

Times assume 20 ticks per second. Covers Neo Vitae and Animus runes; other addons that change rune stats are not included.
