# 1.0.14

## Additions

- The Orb Vitae Link can now be switched off with a redstone signal, letting you gate it off your altar's comparator so it only charges while the altar is full.

## Fixes

- Fixed a crash that could happen when entering a world while wearing Sentient Armor with a movement or eating training upgrade.
- Activated sigils now apply their effects again, instead of doing nothing while toggled on.
- The Ritual Diviner's automatic rune placement works again when activated.
- Sentient Armor now shows its proper texture when worn, instead of a missing/broken texture.
- The Blood Shield (raised by activating an orb in your offhand) now renders correctly and stays directly in front of your view, instead of showing a broken texture and rotating as you turn.
- The Blessed Sacrifice effect now has its status icon, instead of showing a missing texture.
