# 1.1.10

## Fixes

- Crystallum Fractura no longer harvests Crystal Cluster blocks and other decoration, taking the crystals on top with them.
- Crystallum Fractura only speeds up real budding blocks now.
- The Sigil of Bound Treasures now stays open at any distance, instead of closing past nine blocks.
- A container opened with the Sigil of Bound Treasures stays chunk loaded while open, and for a minute after.
- Athanor recipes in JEI no longer swap the names Spiritus Vindicta and Spiritus Invictus.
- The Tabula Vitae no longer crafts from an unbound blood orb. The orb has to be bound, and its network has to hold the Essentia Vitae the recipe asks for.
- Tabula Vitae recipes now charge their full Essentia Vitae cost. Rounding was undercharging most of them, and the slowest ones were running for free.
- The Sanguine Ward stays up while you hold the key and stops the arrows it is meant to stop. It used to flicker away after a moment, block nothing, and keep draining Essentia Vitae anyway.
- Tridents and Throwing Daggers stopped by the Sanguine Ward now drop at its face instead of being destroyed.
- The guide and wiki no longer claim demons drop Spiritus, or that the Sanguine Ward stops melee.
- Dust and gravel textures show the art they were meant to have; the two sets had been swapped since they were drawn.
- Hellforged Dust has its own texture instead of a recolored generic pile, and resource packs can now override it at textures/item/hellforged_dust.png.
- Daemonium spawn eggs no longer darken their artwork with a color tint.
- The colored dungeon block variants sound like stone again instead of wood.
