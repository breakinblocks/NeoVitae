# NeoVitae

> *A magical mod where blood is the currency of power. Harvest it, refine it, weave it into rituals and runes, and bend the world to your will.*

NeoVitae is a blood themed magic mod for Modern Minecraft. You begin with a knife and a wound. From those first crimson drops, you'll build a multiblock altar capable of devouring entire ecosystems, network your soul to a hundred different reservoirs, forge demons into tools, and crack open a dimension whose only inhabitants want you dead.
