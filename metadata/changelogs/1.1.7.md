# 1.1.7

## Fixes

- A Spiritus Gem left in the Hellfire Forge's gem slot no longer loses Spiritus to crafts it did not power.
- Lex Vitae area mining no longer triggers on grass and other blocks that break instantly.
- The Torment Nexus now yields the reward loot that bound trial spawners eject, on each spawner's own cooldown.
- The Sigil of Bound Treasures tooltip now describes what the sigil actually does.
- The Torment Nexus no longer lets riders escape when it binds a spawner that spawns mobs riding other mobs.
- The Teleposition Array entry in the guide no longer sits on top of Fierce Strike, and is back with the other arrays.
- Lex Vitae area mining now respects protected blocks, so it can no longer cut through dungeon walls or claimed land.
- Your arm swings again when breaking blocks or attacking while holding a sigil.
- Fixed a large frame rate drop near active rituals, altars, forges and athanors, caused by their looping sounds being restarted every tick.
- Looping block sounds that failed to start no longer stay silent for the rest of the session.
- The guide's Spiritus page is updated to state only armor and shields can be infused.
- The guide's Tome of Peritia page now lists the right ingredients.
- The guide now calls the Orb of Vitae by its proper name throughout (internal consistency fix).

## Changes

- The Hellfire Forge now marks its progress bar red and says how much Spiritus is missing when a craft is stalled, instead of sitting idle with no explanation.
- That warning also points out that a gem placed in a crafting slot powers the craft instead of the one in the gem slot.
- Hellforged Ingots, Raw Demonite, and Demonite Ore now count for other mods' recipes that accept any ingot, raw material, or ore.
- The Torment Nexus can now bind Ender IO Powered Spawners, harvesting them at whatever speed their installed capacitor allows. Only applies when Ender IO is installed.
- The guide's Alchemy Arrays chapter is rearranged into separate groups for arrays, sigils and sentient gear, so its connecting lines no longer cross each other.
- A new keybind opens the guide straight to the entry for whatever item you are pointing at, in your inventory or in hand. It starts unbound; set it in Controls.

## Translations

- Spanish, French, and Romanian translation updates.
