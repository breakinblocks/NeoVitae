# 1.1.52

## Fixes

- Fixed the Sanguine Crafter not recognizing Auto mode recipes when matching ingredients share a slot, such as items inserted by AE2 autocrafting.
- Fixed the Lex Vitae being held backwards, with axe, hoe, and sword blades facing the player. (#234)

## Additions

- Press Ctrl+C in a Hellfire Forge, Tabula Vitae, Athanor, or Sanguine Crafter menu to copy its side settings, and Ctrl+V in another of the same machine to paste them. (#215)
- Added the Ritual of Hellforging, which upgrades Sentient Armor, Sentient tools, and the Lex Vitae using a Prismatic Spiritus Gem and a Hellforged Ingot. (#151)
- Hellforged armor matches Netherite, and a Hellforged chestplate holds 700 Upgrade Points and trains 1.5x faster.
- Hellforged tools work like Netherite without Spiritus, deal 30% more damage, use less Spiritus, and keep gaining power past 4,000 Spiritus with diminishing returns up to 32,000.
- A Hellforged Lex Vitae grows faster and can switch between Fortune and Silk Touch.
- Hellforged values can be changed in the server config.
- Added the Tome of Deus Sanguinis, a 10-level Sentient upgrade for Hellforged chestplates that grants Blood Siphon and Blood Shield and trains as your network spends Essentia Vitae.
- Hitting another player with Blood Siphon now inflicts Anima Severance for 10 seconds, which stops their network gaining Essentia Vitae and doubles its costs. It cannot be cured.
- Added a client option to choose which Blood Siphon streams are drawn: offhand Orb of Vitae kills only (default), all, or none.

## Balance

- Blood Siphon now converts a percentage of the damage you deal, and Blood Shield prevents a percentage of the damage you take. Both cap at 95%, which can be changed in the server config.
- The Sigil of the Damned now grants 25 Blood Siphon, and Blood Siphon gives more Essentia Vitae per hit.

## Changes

- Reworked the Training Bracelet: it now lists every trainable Sentient Armor upgrade with its current level and a cap you set with - and +, saves each change immediately, and no longer needs Upgrade Tomes. (#218)
- Redrew the explosive charges and twelve status effect icons with new art.
- Removed the Deep Shaped Charge and the Reinforced Deforester, Veinmine, and Fungal Charges. Existing ones become the Augmented Shaped Charge or the matching standard charge.
- The Deforester, Veinmine, and Fungal Charges now reach 256 blocks and accept Fortunate II.

## Translations

- Updated Simplified Chinese translation, thanks to ChuijkYahus (breakinblocks/NeoVitae#233).
