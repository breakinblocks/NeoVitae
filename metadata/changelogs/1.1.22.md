# 1.1.22

## Fixes

- Raw Demonite Blocks now have the common raw hellforged storage tags on both the block and item. Fixes [#141](https://github.com/breakinblocks/NeoVitae/issues/141).
- Spiritus Gems no longer show duplicate spiritus amounts and types in their tooltips; the stored amount, capacity, and type are each shown once. Fixes [#140](https://github.com/breakinblocks/NeoVitae/issues/140).
- Liquified Experience arrays no longer duplicate or destroy experience when an inventory rejects an updated Experience Tome.
- Converting Liquified Experience back into stored XP now leaves fractional XP amounts in the tank instead of discarding them.
- Full Experience Tomes no longer freeze the game when displaying their stored level. Deposits and withdrawals also respect XP storage limits without overflowing.
- The Sigil of Holding now saves changes made to contained sigils during block use, air use, and inventory ticks, including bound destinations, rainbow colors, and upkeep deactivation.
- Athanors now consume the final durability point of their tools, allowing spent tools to move to the output slots correctly.
- The first catalyst applied to a Spiritus Crystal now sets its conversion rate correctly, and its speed boost expires when the catalyst reserve runs out.
- Powering a master routing node with redstone now disables transfers through its network.
- Teleposers now move non-player entities between dimensions correctly and preserve mounted passengers during transfers.
- Teleposers can now link to a teleposer at the same coordinates in another dimension.
