# 1.1.54

## Additions

- The Ritual of Overgrowth can grow Saturated Tau without a creature standing on the crop. With Spiritus Nihilum in the chunk it feeds Tau in range itself, for 100 EV and 0.5 Nihilum per growth stage. breakinblocks/NeoVitae#245
- Dawn of the New Moon scoops from the first block below the master stone, so a circle built in the air lifts the terrain beneath it. breakinblocks/NeoVitae#243

## Changes

- Sentient swords, axes, pickaxes, shovels and scythes are drawn larger when seen in third person. breakinblocks/NeoVitae#240
- Tabula Vitae, Hellfire Forge and Athanor blocks that are sitting idle use far less server time. breakinblocks/NeoVitae#241

## Fixes

- Added a guard against overflow for mods/addons that add very large ev limits.
- The Athanor no longer destroys an item when it removes the item's last enchantment with only one output slot free. breakinblocks/NeoVitae#247
- The Athanor no longer creates extra items when an output tops off a nearly full stack.
- The guide, wiki and ritual info for the Deus Endless Quarry describe its terrain modes instead of Spiritus effects it does not use. breakinblocks/NeoVitae#246
- The guide says where your blood goes when you use an Orb of Vitae: into a nearby Ara Vitae, or into the orb when there is none. breakinblocks/NeoVitae#242
- The guide's Ritual of Overgrowth page lists the right effect for each Spiritus.
