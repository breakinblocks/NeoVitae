# 1.0.26

## Additions

- Diamond, Emerald, Lapis Lazuli, Nether Quartz, and Amethyst now have full material processing chains like the metals. Cut their ore (or Amethyst Clusters) into fragments with a cutting fluid in the Athanor, then work the fragments up through gravel and dust and back into the gem.

## Changes

- The Torment Nexus now keeps suppressing nearby spawners even when it runs out of Essentia Vitae. Its harvesting pauses until EV is available again, but the spawners it covers stay quiet in the meantime.
- The Ritual Diviner's ritual list is now sorted alphabetically by name, so it's easier to find the one you want.

## Fixes

- Fixed a stray texture file whose name caused an "Invalid path in pack" error while loading resources.
- Ritual structure previews placed from the Scriptura Vitae now anchor the block you click on as the Master Ritual Stone, instead of appearing offset up and to the side.
