# 1.0.14

## Fixes

- Activated sigils now apply their effects again, instead of doing nothing while toggled on.
- The Ritual Diviner's automatic rune placement works again when activated.
- Sentient Armor now shows its proper texture when worn, instead of a missing/broken texture.
- The Blood Shield (raised by activating an orb in your offhand) now renders correctly instead of as a broken texture.
- The Blessed Sacrifice effect now has its status icon, instead of showing a missing texture.
