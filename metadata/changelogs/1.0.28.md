# 1.0.28

## Additions

- A Spiritus Gem placed in the Hellfire Forge now steadily draws Spiritus from the surrounding Aura, keeping it topped up so the Forge can craft on its own. Feed the local Aura and it runs unattended.
- Neo Vitae is now available in Brazilian Portuguese.

## Fixes

- The Master Routing Node's energy rate field now shows your configured FE/t when you reopen the menu, instead of resetting to 500.
- The upgrade slot tooltips in the Master Routing Node no longer overlap into unreadable text when an upgrade is installed in the slot.
