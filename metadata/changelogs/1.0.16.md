# 1.0.16

## Additions

- Item routing node filters can now match by data components. Shift-right-click an item in a filter slot to open a picker and choose which of its components must match, letting you filter a single multi-variant item (such as a mod's conduit that stores its type in a component) down to just one variant.

## Changes

- The Cry of the Forsaken Soul ritual is now called The Ritual of Lost Souls, matching the name used in the guide book and wiki.
- Runes, Bloodstones, and the capstone blocks used to build higher-tier altars can now be referenced as a group by recipes, automation, and other mods, the same way their placed forms already could.
- Dungeon Ore has been renamed to Demonite Ore.

## Fixes

- The Hellforged Trim Ingot can be applied at a smithing table again. The smithing table was rejecting it as a valid trim material ("this item can't be upgraded that way").
- Several advancement names and descriptions (the Tabula slate tier advancements) were showing their raw translation keys instead of readable text.
- The Neo Vitae advancements tab now shows its background again instead of a broken texture.
- The light-blue connection beams between routing nodes and their master routing node now render again. The brief link animation played when connecting, but the persistent beams were not showing.
- The altar preview in the guide book now lines up with the block you anchor it to, instead of appearing one block too high, and no longer shows required bedrock blocks beneath the corner caps; the pillar bodies (any solid block) are left out of the preview, as the entry text already explains.
- The Weak Activation Crystal can no longer activate advanced rituals. It was able to start every ritual, including ones meant to require the Awakened Activation Crystal (such as the Torment Nexus); those now correctly require the Awakened crystal.
- The Hellforged armor trim now renders correctly when applied to armor, and is crafted from a Hellforged Ingot to match its name.
- The guide now says to lay deepslate (not smooth stone) on the altar to make a Tabula Rasa, matching the actual recipe.
- Demonite Ore now drops Raw Demonite when mined, or the ore block itself with Silk Touch. It was dropping both the ore block and Raw Demonite at once, ignoring Silk Touch.
- The Tabula Vitae now consumes its ingredients when crafting. Recipes were finishing and producing their output without using up the materials placed in the table.
- Breaking a Tabula Vitae no longer voids its contents. Items left inside are now dropped no matter which part of the table you break.
- The Tabula Vitae recipe pages in JEI now show the correct Blood Orb. Recipes that need an Apprentice Orb were showing the lower Weak Orb, which the table would not accept.
- Curios slots now accept their items again. The Sentient Training Bracelet, Tome of Peritia, sigils, Spiritus Gems, and the Spiritus Aura Gauge could not be placed into their charm, bracelet, or necklace slots; they can now all be equipped.
- Upgrade Tomes found in mine chests now carry their intended Living Armor upgrade and experience. They were dropping blank, with no upgrade attached and nothing to apply when used.
