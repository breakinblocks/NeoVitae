# 1.1.10

## Changes

- New block: the Spirit Accumulator, crafted in the Athanor. The floating crystal draws spiritus out its chunk and stores up to 1,000 of a single Aspect.
- A new Accumulator is unattuned and gathers nothing until you set it. Right-click to cycle the Aspect and crouch-click to lock it, or right-click with a spiritus crystal to lock straight to that Aspect. It skims anything above 30 in its chunk, so a single crystal burned nearby is enough to feed it.
- To retune an Accumulator, crouch-click to vent 50 back into the chunk; once it is empty the next crouch-click releases the lock.
- Spiritus now travels the routing network. A Spirit Accumulator links in as an input node, and Output Nodes gained a Spiritus tab that keeps their own chunk stocked with a chosen Aspect and amount, no attached container needed.
- Jade shows a Spirit Accumulator's stored Aspect and amount.
- The guide book and wiki cover the Spirit Accumulator and spiritus routing.
- A new setting in config/neovitae-startup.toml can turn off Essentia Vitae soaking into blocks, for packs with a mod whose blocks cannot cope with the extra blockstate.

## Fixes

- The Ritual Configurator now rejects a first corner that is out of range, instead of accepting it and then refusing every second corner you pick.
- Crystallum Fractura no longer harvests Crystal Cluster blocks and other decoration, taking the crystals on top with them.
- Crystallum Fractura only speeds up real budding blocks now.
- The Sigil of Bound Treasures now stays open at any distance, instead of closing past nine blocks.
- A container opened with the Sigil of Bound Treasures stays chunk loaded while open, and for a minute after.
- Athanor recipes in JEI no longer swap the names Spiritus Vindicta and Spiritus Invictus.
- The Tabula Vitae no longer crafts from an unbound blood orb. The orb has to be bound, and its network has to hold the Essentia Vitae the recipe asks for.
- Tabula Vitae recipes now charge their full Essentia Vitae cost. Rounding was undercharging most of them, and the slowest ones were running for free.
- The Sanguine Ward stays up while you hold the key and stops the arrows it is meant to stop. It used to flicker away after a moment, block nothing, and keep draining Essentia Vitae anyway.
- Tridents and Throwing Daggers stopped by the Sanguine Ward now drop at its face instead of being destroyed.
- The guide and wiki no longer claim demons drop Spiritus, or that the Sanguine Ward stops melee.
- Dust and gravel textures show the art they were meant to have; the two sets had been swapped since they were drawn.
- Hellforged Dust has its own texture instead of a recolored generic pile, and resource packs can now override it at textures/item/hellforged_dust.png.
- Daemonium spawn eggs no longer darken their artwork with a color tint.
- The colored dungeon block variants sound like stone again instead of wood.
- The Ritual Configurator now edits the stone whose panel you opened. With two ritual stones near each other it could validate your corners against the wrong stone, rejecting every pick as invalid or out of range.
- Blocks broken by rituals no longer set off area mining tools like the Lex Vitae around whatever the ritual broke.
