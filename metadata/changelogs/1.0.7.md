# 1.0.7

## Enchanting

- Spiritus aspect crystal clusters (Raw, Ruina, Nihilum, Vindicta, Invictus) now provide enchanting power to nearby Enchanting Tables, at **double a bookshelf's** strength.
- The Crystal Cluster and Crystal Cluster Brick blocks (the maximum-tier Ara Vitae capstones) also provide enchanting power, at **2.5x a bookshelf's** strength.
- Both act like bookshelves for placement: they must sit two blocks from the table with an open gap between.
- These blocks now carry a tooltip noting they can act as an Enchanting Table amplifier.

## Sentient Armour

- Every Sentient upgrade and downgrade tome now appears as its own entry in JEI, with an info page explaining what the upgrade does, how it is trained (or that it is applied directly), and how to obtain the physical tome (dungeon loot, the Sentient Extraction ritual, or combining duplicates). Downgrade tomes explain their curse and that they come from the Sentient Downgrade ritual. A JEI subtype interpreter distinguishes the tomes so each shows separately instead of collapsing into one generic Upgrade Tome.

## Fixes

- The **Body Builder** Sentient upgrade now trains correctly. It was reading the wrong direction of food change through an unreliable vanilla value, so it effectively never gained experience from eating. It now tracks your food level directly and gains experience equal to the food restored whenever you eat.
- Fixed Sentient Armour attribute modifiers (Knockback Resistance, Armor, Health, and the like) stacking endless duplicate copies of themselves. The chestplate rebuilt its modifiers by copying its current set and then re-adding the upgrade bonuses on top, so every time the armour's data changed (for example, each block mined) another copy was appended, producing absurd values such as +1400% Knockback Resistance. The rebuild now starts from the armour's base modifiers, so upgrade bonuses apply exactly once. Existing affected armour self-corrects the next time it is re-equipped or its upgrades change.
