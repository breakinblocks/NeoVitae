# 1.1.9

## Changes

- Essentia Vitae now soaks into blocks the way water does. Slabs, stairs, fences, walls, lanterns and the rest hold the blood in place instead of pushing it out, and an empty bucket takes it back.
- Blocks from other mods hold Essentia Vitae as well, so long as they are built on a vanilla block. Anything more unusual can be added on request.
- Essentia Vitae keeps farmland hydrated, so a blood pool feeds a farm the way a water pool does.
- Essentia Vitae flows more slowly and searched for a drop less aggressively, so it spreads like something thicker than water.
- The Ritual of the Overgrowth grows crops about 25% faster. It works the same land on the same schedule, it just coaxes more growth out of each pass.
- The Ritual of the Harvest costs less Essentia Vitae to keep running.
- Crystallum Fractura costs about a 20% less Essentia Vitae to keep running.
- The Tome of Peritia opens a proper window instead of making you click over and over. Store or withdraw 1, 5, 10, 50, 100 levels at a time, or everything at once.
- New fluid: Liquified Experience, and a new Array of Liquified Experience to go with it. Inscribe the array on a chest, put a tank beside it, and any Tomes of Peritia in the chest drain into the tank. Feed the array a redstone signal and it runs the other way, filling the tomes back up from the tank.
- Packs that already have an experience fluid from another mod can point the array at it in the server config.
- Every alchemy array texture is now the same small size, which trims a good chunk off the texture atlas.

## Fixes

- Slimes of Vitae no longer suffocate where they spawn or shove you around from a distance. Their hitbox was almost four times their visible size and now matches it.
- Trial spawners claimed by a Torment Nexus no longer throw their reward loot onto the floor. It goes into the chest with everything else the nexus collects.
- A Torment Nexus no longer needs a player standing nearby to work a trial spawner. It runs on its own schedule for as long as the chunk is loaded.
- Spawners claimed by a Torment Nexus stay held back through a server restart. They no longer get a free wave of mobs while the world is still loading.
- Routing node connection beams no longer hang in the air pointing at nodes that have been destroyed. Nodes now tidy up broken links themselves, and a beam is only drawn when both ends can be seen to still be there.
- An activation crystal that has not been bound to you now says so when you use it on a Master Ritual Stone, instead of leaving you to guess.
- A ritual that refuses to activate for any other reason now tells you rather than doing nothing at all.
