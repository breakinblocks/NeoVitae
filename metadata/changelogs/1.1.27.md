# 1.1.27

## Features

- Added a server config option to turn off Blood and Demon Lantern spawn suppression.
- The Ritual of Meteo guide entry now lists every meteor your world recognizes, including ones added by modpacks, with its offering, cost, size and contents.
- Master Ritual Stones and Ritual Stones now resist explosions as well as obsidian does.

## Fixes

- Crystal catalysts now convert a fully grown crystal cluster instead of breaking a crystal off it when you carry a filled Spiritus Gem.
- Spectral Sight now outlines nearby creatures through walls.
- Blood Lanterns no longer cause lag spikes when many chunks load at once, such as when a map mod renders terrain.
- Fixed a possible crash when other mods spawn animals near a Blood Lantern.
- Demon Lanterns near unloaded terrain no longer load extra chunks during spawn checks.
