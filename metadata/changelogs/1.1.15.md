# 1.1.15

## Additions

- EMI support. Every Neo Vitae recipe category now shows in EMI, for packs that use it instead of JEI.

## Changes

- Dusk Ritual Stones are now Tenebrae Ritual Stones, and Dawn Ritual Stones are now Deus Ritual Stones. The Dusk Inscription Tool and Ritual Diviner [Dusk] are renamed to match. Existing worlds carry over; placed stones and stored tools keep working.
- Improved Ritual of Enchanted Vitae documentation

## Fixes

- The Ritual of Enchanted Vitae now properly requires Tenebrae Ritual Stones.
