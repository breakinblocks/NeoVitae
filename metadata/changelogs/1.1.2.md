# 1.1.2

## Fixes

- Fixed being kicked to the title screen with a connection error when joining a world.
- The aura gauge and other on-screen indicators now hide along with the rest of the interface when you press F1.
- Recipe details in JEI (ritual costs and rune requirements, Athanor, Hellfire Forge, Tabula Vitae and other categories) are shown again instead of appearing blank.
- The guide book's Ara Vitae multiblock preview no longer shows a correctly-built altar as missing its capstones.
- Fixed the Master Routing Node showing a garbled or negative transfer rate and ceiling once its throttle is raised past ~32,000 with several stack upgrades.
- Explosions (TNT, creepers, and the like) can no longer break the indestructible blocks that make up the dungeon structure.
