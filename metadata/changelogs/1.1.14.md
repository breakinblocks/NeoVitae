# 1.1.14

## Fixes

- Packs upgrading from 1.1.10 or 1.1.11 kept Essentia Vitae soaking into blocks switched on, which could stop the game from starting. It is switched back off for them, unless it was turned on by hand.
- The log now says when Essentia Vitae soaking is on, and what to change if the game will not start.
- Smelting or blasting Hellforged Dust and Raw Demonite now gives experience. Both were giving none at all.
