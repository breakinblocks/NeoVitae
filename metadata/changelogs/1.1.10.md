# 1.1.10

## Fixes

- Crystallum Fractura no longer harvests Crystal Cluster blocks and other decoration, taking the crystals on top with them.
- Crystallum Fractura only speeds up real budding blocks now.
- The Sigil of Bound Treasures now stays open at any distance, instead of closing past nine blocks.
- A container opened with the Sigil of Bound Treasures stays chunk loaded while open, and for a minute after.
- Athanor recipes in JEI no longer swap the names Spiritus Vindicta and Spiritus Invictus.
