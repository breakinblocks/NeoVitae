# 1.1.51

## Fixes

- Fixed the Sigil of Suppression briefly removing waterlogged blocks near you, which reset anything stored in them: chests spilled their items and NauTec Cultivated Prismarine Crystals turned natural. Like the Dome of Suppression, it now leaves waterlogged blocks alone.
- Fixed Spirit Accumulators immediately reabsorbing vented Spiritus. (#228)
- Fixed the Mason ritual failing to replace water and lava. (#221)
- Fixed Sentient Armor Curios sockets losing their slots after login or death. (#226)
- Fixed the Endless Quarry deleting Allthemodium ore when automated mining is blocked. (#232)
- Fixed breakable exterior walls in the Hidden Realm crypt. (#216)
- Fixed overlapping ingredients and tool slots in guidebook Athanor recipes. (#213)
- Fixed missing upgrade, Curios socket, and Zanite material names. (#214)
- Removed outdated Blood Magic routing screenshots from the guidebook. (#207)

## Additions

- Added Sanguine Crafter Packing and Unpacking modes for reversible 2×2 and 3×3 crafting recipes, without a template. Both directions require existing recipes that return the exact original items and quantity. (#231)
- Added the Deus Ritual of Enchanted Vitae: combine conflicting enchantments and upgrade book-selected enchantments beyond their normal limits, up to the game-supported ceiling. Book consumption is configurable. (#230)
