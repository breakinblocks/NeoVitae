# 1.1.23

## Additions

- Added the `neovitae:meteor` item tag, filled automatically with every meteor catalyst from the loaded recipes, including ones added by datapacks or KubeJS.

## Fixes

- The Applied Energistics meteor now contains Certus Quartz Blocks and Blocks of Quartz in its outer layer. Fixes [#142](https://github.com/breakinblocks/NeoVitae/issues/142).
- EMI now shows the correct Essentia Vitae amounts for Athanor and disenchanting recipes instead of values 81 times too large.
- The EMI Meteor Ritual category now uses the Master Ritual Stone as its icon, matching JEI.
