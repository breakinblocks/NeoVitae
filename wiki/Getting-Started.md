# Getting Started

> *So you wish to walk the path of Vitaemancy. Very well.*

This is a map of the journey; from your first tentative cuts to forces that reshape reality itself. Read it once for orientation, then begin.

## Tier 0, The First Altar

Your first act as a Vitaemancer is to construct an **Ara Vitae** and a **Sacrificial Knife**.

1. Place a single Ara Vitae block on flat stone.
2. Stand beside it with a Sacrificial Knife in hand.
3. Right-click yourself with the knife. You bleed; the altar drinks. The blood refines into **Essentia Vitae (EV)**, which pools in the altar's basin.

With EV in the basin, the altar can craft:

- **Novicius Orb of Vitae** (the first Blood Orb). Bind it to yourself by right-clicking with it in hand.
- **Tabula Rasa**. The substrate for almost every sigil.
- **Throwing Daggers**. Early ranged weapon and the seed of the Spiritus economy.

See **[Ara Vitae and Runes](Ara-Vitae-and-Runes)** for the full tier diagram.

## Tier 0, The Tabula Vitae

The **Tabula Vitae** is your first alchemical bench. With an Orb of Vitae placed inside, it draws EV from your Anima to brew:

- **Arcane Scribe Tools** for drawing alchemy arrays
- Reagents for sigil crafting
- Early anointments for your blades
- 2× ore processing recipes
- A long list of utility reagents

See **[Tabula Vitae, Flasks and Anointments](Tabula-Vitae-Flasks-and-Anointments)**.

## Tier 0, Alchemy Arrays

Strike the ground with an **Arcane Scribe Tool** and a circle of power awakens. The **Alchemy Array** accepts two offerings; some combinations yield crafted objects (your first sigil, the **Divination Sigil**, comes from here), others invoke immediate effects on the world.

See **[Alchemy Arrays](Alchemy-Arrays)** for the full array catalog.

## Tier 0, The Hellfire Forge

The **Hellfire Forge** is fueled by **Spiritus**, a residue you tear from creatures wounded by your Throwing Daggers. (A **Sentient Sword** harvests it far more efficiently.) From this forge come **Spiritus Gems**, **Sentient Tools**, **Explosive Charges**, and more daggers.

See **[Hellfire Forge and Sentient Equipment](Hellfire-Forge-and-Sentient-Equipment)** and **[Spiritus, Aspects and Crystals](Spiritus-Aspects-and-Crystals)**.

## Tier I, Upgrade Runes

The second altar tier unlocks **Upgrade Runes** and a wider sigil catalog. Hold an **Orb of Vitae** in your off-hand while killing creatures to harvest EV directly into your Anima, sparing your own veins.

Continue expanding the altar; its hunger grows with your ambition.

## Tier I, Potioncrafting

If altar construction starts to drag, the **potioncrafting system** opens at Tier I. Prepare **Alchemy Flasks** or tip **Amethyst Throwing Daggers** with dozens of effects. A Vitaemancer with a well-stocked flask belt is never truly in danger.

## Tier II, Rituals and Sentient Armour

The true scope of Vitaemancy reveals itself here. You gain the ability to inscribe **Rituals** via the Master Ritual Stone, and to forge **Sentient Armour**, equipment that grows stronger through the trials you endure.

Upgrade both the altar and your **Ritual Diviner** to unlock more potent rites.

See **[Rituals](Rituals)** and **[Hellfire Forge and Sentient Equipment](Hellfire-Forge-and-Sentient-Equipment)**.

## Tier II, Dungeoneering

Perform the **Edge of the Hidden Realm** ritual and you tear a wound in the veil for limited passage into the **Demon Realm**. There you may find **Tau Fruit**. Cultivate it into **Saturated Tau**, refine it in the Athanor to produce **Weak Blood Shards**, the key to the next tier, stronger anointments, and potion catalysts.

See **[The Endless Dungeon](The-Endless-Dungeon)** and **[Athanor and Materials](Athanor-and-Materials)**.

## Tier III, The Dusk Diviner

Tier III unlocks the **Ritual Diviner [Dusk]** and a vast repertoire of advanced rites. Automate your EV supply, soar in unfettered flight within your sanctum, call down a **meteor** laden with bounty.

## Tier III, Sentient Evolution

Your Sentient Armour may start to feel cramped by its modest point cap. With the Dusk Diviner, perform the **Ritual of Sentient Evolution** to raise your armour's capacity from **100** to **300** points. The **Sound of the Cleansing Soul** strips upgrades into **Upgrade Tomes** you can copy into a **Training Bracelet** or re-apply selectively. Surplus tomes fuel the **Penance of the Leaden Soul**, which inscribes **Downgrades**, negative-cost effects that free room for greater upgrades.

See **[Living Armor](Living-Armor)** for the full upgrade/downgrade tree.

## Tier III, Aspected Spiritus

The **Ritual Tinkerer** and the five **Aspects of Spiritus** (Raw, Ruina, Nihilum, Vindicta, Invictus) refine your rituals and reshape how your Sentient Tools behave.

See **[Spiritus, Aspects and Crystals](Spiritus-Aspects-and-Crystals)**.

## Tier III, The Endless Realm

The **Pathway to the Endless Realm** tears open a permanent gate. Come armed and armoured; the realm does not suffer the unprepared. Delve deep enough and you may unearth **Demonite Ore**, the capstone material for the ultimate altar tiers, alongside the **Dominus Orb of Vitae**.

## Tier IV, Devastating Rites

Tier IV grants the most destructive rituals and the most potent crafts in all of Vitaemancy. With fortune, you may recover **Intricate Hellforged Parts** from the deep dungeon; combined with **Netherite Scrap**, they double the power of every rune on your altar.

## Tier V, The Pinnacle

The ultimate Ara Vitae. **Crystal Clusters** crown its pillars. An immense ring of nineteen runes per side girds its foundation. Capacity and crafting power without equal. What you do with that power is yours to decide.
