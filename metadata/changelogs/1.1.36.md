# 1.1.36

## Changes

- The Gathering drops items on top of its chest once the chest is full, instead of carrying them to the owner.

## Fixes

- Incense Altar paths reach only as far as their material allows: wood paths 3 rings, stone 5, worn stone 7 and obsidian 11. Dirt Paths and Stone Bricks count for the first ring only.
- Obsidian paths reach far enough for the Incense Altar's highest bonus, 450%.
