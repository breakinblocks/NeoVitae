# 1.0.19

## Additions

- Earn a new advancement for imbuing a tool or piece of armor with Blood Mending at the Hellfire Forge.

## Fixes

- Spiritus souls dropped by the demonic mobs in the dungeon now carry their Spiritus value again. They were dropping completely empty, so there was nothing to absorb when you used one or fed it into a Spiritus gem.
- Fixed a crash that struck when looking at an active Master Ritual Stone while carrying a Seer Sigil. The Sigil's ritual-area outline is now drawn correctly instead of bringing the game down.
- Spiritus gems worn in a Curios slot now collect and spend Spiritus again. Only gems carried in your main inventory were being filled or drawn from; an equipped gem was ignored entirely.
- An Ara Vitae now pulses a redstone signal when a transmutation finishes, so a redstone lamp or noteblock placed directly beneath the altar reacts as the guide describes.
- A Blood Orb that already held EV no longer acts as an infinite source when placed in an Ara Vitae. It used to fill the altar and your network without ever being drained; its stored EV is now properly consumed as it transfers.
- The Torment Nexus no longer drains your Soul Network uncontrollably. The EV cost to run it is now capped per operation, so even a sprawling spawner array can only ask so much of your network at once. Kills past that point still happen, still drop their loot, still grant experience, and still feed their EV into the altar.
