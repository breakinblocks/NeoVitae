# 1.1.14

## Changes

- Essentia Vitae no longer soaks into slabs, stairs, fences and other waterloggable blocks. The experimental feature was off by default already, and it cost every world several seconds of load time whether it was on or not. On a large pack that was over ten seconds. Blocks holding Essentia Vitae in an older world come back empty; nothing else changes.

## Fixes

- Smelting or blasting Hellforged Dust and Raw Demonite now gives experience. Both were giving none at all.
- A spawner or trial spawner picked up with a Spiritus Gem keeps its mob and settings when you place it back down. It was coming back blank.
- The Tabula Vitae says why it is sitting idle. Hover the progress bar and it tells you whether the items make nothing, the orb is missing, unbound or too low a tier, the network is out of Essentia Vitae, or the output slot is full.
