# 1.1.16

## Changes

- Trimmed Neo Vitae's block textures, cutting its share of the texture atlas by about a third. No visual change.
- The Torment Nexus now reaps as the ritual owner for Apotheosis, so gear and gems drop at the owner's world tier instead of always the lowest one.
- The owner's world tier is remembered after they log off, so a Nexus left running keeps dropping at their tier.

## Fixes

- The Tabula Vitae is now solid across its full width. Clicks used to fall through the middle onto the block behind, and breaking one could break the wall instead.
- Items with no durability no longer get pushed straight out of the Athanor tool slot into the output.
- A worn out Athanor tool is now handed back in the output instead of being destroyed, so it can be repaired.
- The Athanor says why it is sitting idle. Hover the progress arrow and it tells you whether the output is full, the tool is worn out, or the chunk is short on Spiritus.
- The Ritual of Speed shared its rune pattern with the Ritual of the Phantom Bridge, so building one could give you the other. Speed now takes two Tenebrae runes beside the master stone, and a Tenebrae Ritual Diviner to build.
- The Ritual of Speed now fires in the direction you build it. It always fired north before, whichever way you laid the circle out.
- Existing Speed circles will not run until the two new runes are added.

## Pack Devs

- Athanor recipes can now leave out the tool entirely. A recipe with no tool only runs when the tool slot is empty, so it will not clash with tool recipes that share the same inputs.
- Custom Athanor tools still need adding to the neovitae:athanor_tool tag before they can be put in the tool slot.
