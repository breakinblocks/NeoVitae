# 1.0.22

## Additions

- Brilliance for Living Armour can now be crafted as an Upgrade Tome in the Tabula Vitae from a diamond, a written book, a netherite ingot, and a shulker shell (needs a Master Blood Orb and 10,000 Essentia Vitae). Apply the tome to your armour to gain a level of Brilliance, up to four.

## Changes

- The Living Armour upgrade that used to show as "Forgotten" is now called "Brilliance" everywhere, matching the guide and wiki.
- The Sigil of Bound Treasures can now bind to and remotely open modded containers, such as Sophisticated Storage and Iron Chests, not just vanilla ones.
- Reorganized the Rituals chapter: ritual stones, the diviner, and activation crystals now come first, and the rituals are grouped by the altar tier needed to reach them.
- Reorganized the Spiritus chapter so the Vas Maleficum, which generates the aura, comes before the Crystallarium that depends on it.
- The Ritual of Meteo is now nondestructive: the summoned meteor fills only open or replaceable space and leaves existing terrain and builds untouched, and its impact no longer blasts a crater.

## Fixes

- The Teleposer Focus is now transmuted from an Eye of Ender on a Tier 3 Ara Vitae. It previously used an Ender Pearl, which clashed with the Blood Pearl recipe (also an Ender Pearl), so the altar could not reliably produce either.
- The meteor called down by the Ritual of Meteo now shows its proper texture instead of a missing-texture placeholder.
- The Foreman's treasure now drops a usable Upgrade Tome (Curios Sockets, Elytra, or Gilded) instead of a blank one that granted nothing when applied.
- Applying Blood Mending at the Hellfire Forge now requires a minimum of 400 Spiritus and consumes 200. The two values were swapped, so the forge asked for only 200 to start but tried to drain 400.
