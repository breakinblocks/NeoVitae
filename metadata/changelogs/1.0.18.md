# 1.0.18

## Fixes

- The Torment Nexus is now reliably net positive on a well-fed altar. Each simulated kill still deposits its sacrifice EV (boosted by your altar's Sacrifice runes) into the altar, but once the altar's buffer is full the excess now flows straight into the bound Blood Orb instead of being thrown away, so high-throughput spawner setups actually bank the EV they generate.
- Fixed the Torment Nexus over-spending the network. Its per-kill EV charge was checked against your network balance as it stood at the start of each cycle rather than as it was actually being spent, so a fast spawner array could drain far more EV in a single tick than you could afford. The cost is now measured against your live balance and stops the moment you can no longer pay.
