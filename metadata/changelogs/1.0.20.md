# 1.0.20

## Changes

- An Orb of Vitae placed in an Ara Vitae now stops once it has emptied its stored Essentia Vitae into the altar, instead of immediately draining that Essentia Vitae back out into your Soul Network. Take the orb off and place it again when you want to charge your network from the altar.

## Fixes

- You can now harvest a Spiritus Crystal cluster by right-clicking it with an empty hand while carrying more than 512 Spiritus, as the guide describes. The empty-handed harvest never worked before; you had to be holding an item. Both work now.
