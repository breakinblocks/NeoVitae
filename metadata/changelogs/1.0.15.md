# 1.0.15

## Fixes

- Sentient pickaxes, axes, shovels, and the Lex Vitae now apply their spiritus mining-speed bonus while you simply hold them, keeping it current as your spiritus rises and falls. The Lex Vitae was gaining no mining-speed bonus at all, and the others only refreshed theirs after a right-click or attack. The bonus is now also listed on each tool's tooltip (hold Shift).
